By Team Unity.X (Sheenam, Madhu, Claudia, Saba, and Tushar)

What is it? Wakie is an alarm app with a twist for people who just can't stop hitting snooze. We replaced the boring off button with actual wake-up challenges that force you to get up, move around, and kick-start your every morning.

The Challenges:

* **Shaky Shaky:** You have to dance for 20 seconds to shut the alarm up. We use YOLO pose detection to make sure you are actually moving.


* **Pickey Pickey:** A morning scavenger hunt. The app asks for a random item, like a spoon, watch, or pen. Find it and show it to the camera (using YOLO object detection) to stop the ringing.


* **Blah Blah:** You have to talk for 30 seconds straight. We record your sleepy morning rambles and save them for you to listen to later.
